How to play

  1. Level 0 is plain tic-tac-toe. X goes first.
  2. Win Level 0 and your mark becomes the center square of Level 1, a brand-new, bigger board. The other player picks first there.
  3. On Level 1 and above you don't just place a mark. You pick an empty square, the camera zooms in, and a fresh Level 0 game decides who owns it. Whoever picked moves first in that game.
  4. Win three in a row on Level 1 and your mark becomes the center of Level 2, and so on, forever. Every square on every level is won with one Level 0 game.
  5. Glowing squares on the bigger boards would complete a line for that color. The stakes bar at the top tells you what the current game means, and the mini-map on the left shows the live boards and earlier levels. Click a mini board to zoom to it.
  6. Level zoom: use the zoom bar, the trail at the top, your scroll wheel, or the + / − keys to move between levels. Click any claimed square to zoom into the finished game that won it. Press Esc or Back to game to return.
  7. Wheel of games: before every Level 0 game, the winner of the last Level 0 game spins the wheel to pick the game type (X spins first). A title card names the rule, and the badge above the turn bar keeps it on screen.
    • ⭕ Classic Tic-Tac-Toe: plain rules.
    • 👻 Vanishing Marks: you keep only your 3 newest marks. Your 4th makes your oldest vanish (it flickers when it's next; the dots on the badge count your marks). No winner within 18 moves is a stalemate.
    • ⬇️ Gravity: marks fall to the front-left end of their column.
    • 🔀 Shifting Board: every 3 moves the row or column with the arrows slides one space, and marks pushed off the edge wrap around.
    • 🌈 Wild Square: the rainbow star counts for both players and jumps to a new square after every turn.
    • 🙃 Misère: whoever makes three in a row loses.
    Outside Vanishing Marks, a full board with no line is a stalemate. A 🎲 rule pick from the slot machine lets its winner choose the next game type instead of spinning.
  8. Mystery jackpot: every new board hides a jackpot under one square (watch for a little extra sparkle). Land a mark on it to spin the slot machine: ⚡ extra move, 🎭 steal a mark, 💣 bomb a mark or stone, 🛡️ keep 4 marks (Vanishing Marks only), 🎲 rule pick (choose the next game type), 🎆 INSTANT WIN on Level 0 or a free square on big boards... or bust.
  9. Stone squares: a stalemate turns the square it was deciding into stone, which blocks both players. If a whole big board fills with no line, the edges and stone squares clear, corners and center stay as a head start, and the other player picks first.
